How we choose the tips
  • New selective policy: at most one tip per match, with at least 100 prior league games and 10 games per team. Older saved tips keep their original rules. These stricter gates do not establish improved accuracy.
  • New estimates must be between 60% and 85%. We avoid extreme goal lines and reject negative model value when a current supported bookmaker quote is present.
  • Highest model probability ranks first. This is a transparent shortlist rule, not proof of accuracy. Markets can still be correlated.
  • The percentage bar shows estimated probability, not a measured confidence score. Uncalibrated estimates are labelled.
  • Corners use opponent-adjusted counts. A shared-tempo alternative needs a chronological validation gain. Expand a match for corner-data coverage and the full corner report. Missing crosses and score-state corner timestamps receive no invented boost.
  • Tips are saved before kickoff. Won/lost uses final regulation scores or recorded statistics. Unsupported settlements stay pending.
  • Asian handicap percentages mean a full or half win. Refunds and partial losses are shown separately. Archived-model projections are untracked. Handicap fair odds include split-stake outcomes.
  • Dotted odds are model fair prices. Binary market fair odds are calculated as 1 / probability. Bet365 prices, when shown, are saved snapshots rather than live offers.
